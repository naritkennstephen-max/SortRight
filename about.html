About SortRight

The problem

Many households mix biodegradable, recyclable, and hazardous waste in one bag. Garbage piles up, recyclables are lost, and dangerous items like batteries end up in the wrong place.

Our solution

SortRight is a simple website that teaches correct waste segregation. People can search an item, read how to dispose of it, and practice with a timed game.

Who it is for

Students, parents, and barangay households who want clear, quick answers without installing anything.

The four bins

Biodegradable for food and leaves. Recyclable for clean paper, plastic, glass, and metal. Residual for items that can't be reused. Hazardous for batteries, bulbs, paint, and medicine.
